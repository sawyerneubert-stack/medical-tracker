Our Story

Everyday Luxury, Within Reach

KROWN Jewelers began with a simple idea: the jewelry you love most should be the jewelry you actually wear. Not locked away for special occasions, but part of every moment: the commute, the coffee date, the celebration.

We design timeless pieces with the look and feel of fine jewelry, finished in 18K gold and set with brilliant stones, then offer them at prices that make sense. Every piece is quality-checked before it ships to you.
