Frequently Asked Questions

What is moissanite?

Moissanite is a lab-grown gemstone that is extremely hard (9.25 on the Mohs scale, just below diamond) and gives off even more fire and brilliance than a diamond, at a fraction of the price. Our Moissanite Watches have moissanite stones set into the bezel, dial or bracelet; each product page lists exactly where.

Will moissanite pass a diamond tester?

Most standard thermal diamond testers read moissanite as diamond, because both conduct heat very well. Specialised moissanite testers can tell them apart. We always sell moissanite as moissanite.

What are your watches made of?

Our watches have stainless steel cases with a steel, leather, mesh or rubber strap, depending on the style. The case size, movement, strap and water resistance of each watch are listed on its product page.

Will the watch fit my wrist?

Leather and rubber straps have multiple holes to fit most wrists. Mesh straps adjust with a sliding clasp. Link bracelets can be shortened by removing links at any local jeweler or watch repair shop, usually in a few minutes.

Are the watches waterproof?

Every watch is water resistant; the rating is on its product page. 3 ATM handles splashes and rain, 5 ATM is fine for hand-washing and showers, and 10 ATM is suitable for swimming. Don't press the buttons or pull out the crown while the watch is wet.

What about the battery?

Our quartz watches ship with a battery installed, which typically lasts 2–3 years. It can be replaced at any watch repair shop.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Orders can be changed or cancelled within 12 hours of purchase. Email us as soon as possible with your order number.
